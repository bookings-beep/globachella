# 🌍 Globachella
### Coachella 2026, in your timezone.
Every stage. Every set. Live right now. Wherever you are.

**[globachella.drmgrp.nz](https://globachella.drmgrp.nz)**

## Why
YouTube's Coachella livestream shows you the stage name. Not who's actually playing. If you're outside the US you have no idea what time anything is on. Globachella fixes both.

## What it does
- Auto-detects your timezone
- Shows all 7 stages across Friday, Saturday, Sunday
- Displays every set time in your local time
- Tap any card → goes straight to the YouTube livestream
- Live thumbnail from each stage stream as the card background

## Stages
Coachella Stage · Outdoor Theatre · Sahara · Mojave · Gobi · Sonora · Quasar

## Fork it
Single HTML file. No framework. No build step. No login.
Fork it and swap in any festival's schedule.
